HPSA History

How a Sunday pickup game became a Toronto softball league, and every division champion since 2012.

Our story

HPSA began in 2004 as a Sunday-morning pickup game on a field near Rockford and Bathurst. It was a cold start: the first morning was chilly enough that players wore gloves just to hold the metal bats. About fourteen people turned up every Sunday that summer.

The next season, the City of Toronto offered the group Hendon Park East Field on Tuesday nights, and the league still plays there today. Within six weeks a core group of about twenty regulars had formed, and most of them are still playing.

The following winter the group grew to six full teams and was formally established as the Hendon Park Sports Association. The league kept growing: twelve teams by 2007, seventeen by 2008 and twenty-two by 2009. Before long HPSA was a five-division, forty-team league, built on safety, sportsmanship and a shared passion for softball.
