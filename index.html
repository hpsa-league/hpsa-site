About HPSA

The Hendon Park Sports Association (HPSA) is a softball league that plays at Hendon Park and L'Amoreaux Sports Complex, serving players in Toronto and Scarborough. We run Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday night divisions. Check the standings, the game schedule and scores, the playoff brackets, player stats and the league’s history all season long.
